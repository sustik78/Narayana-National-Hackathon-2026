# SACH AI — User Guide & Demonstration Manual

---

## 1. Quick Start Guide

### Step 1: Access the Platform
Open `http://localhost:5173` in your browser.

### Step 2: Choose Your Language & Accessibility Preferences
- **Language**: Toggle between **English**, **हिन्दी (Hindi)**, and **বাংলা (Bengali)** via the top navigation bar.
- **Accessibility**: Click **A+** for Large Text Mode or **Contrast** for High-Contrast Mode (ideal for low-vision or elderly users).

### Step 3: Submit a Financial Claim
- **Voice Recording**: Click the microphone button, grant mic permission, speak your question or forward in natural language, and click Stop.
- **Upload Audio File**: Drag & drop or browse a `.wav`, `.mp3`, `.m4a`, or `.webm` voice note.
- **Pasted Text / Sample Scenarios**: Switch to "Type / Paste Text" or click "Try Sample Scenarios" for instant 1-click test templates.

### Step 4: Review Transcript & Analyze
- Review and edit the transcribed text in the input box if needed.
- Click **"Analyze for Misinformation & Red Flags"**.

### Step 5: Explore the Results
- **Overall Assessment**: Review the Red Flag or Needs Verification badge and the 0–100 Risk Meter.
- **Listen to Spoken Explanation**: Click **"Listen to Explanation"** to hear the audio readout in your selected language.
- **Claim Breakdown**: View individual statements classified by category and trigger words.
- **Official Evidence**: Access direct links to SEBI SCORES, NSDL, BSE/NSE, and 1930 Cybercrime reporting.
