# SACH AI — Hackathon Video Demo Script (3 to 5 Minutes)

**Track E: Misinformation & Content Literacy**  
**SANGYAN Hackathon (SNTC, IIT BHU Varanasi • SEBI • NSDL)**

---

## Shot-by-Shot Demonstration Script

### Scene 1: Introduction & The Problem (0:00 – 0:45)
- **Visual**: SACH AI Homepage with SANGYAN Hackathon badge, clean navy/teal UI, and tagline *"Suniye, Samajhiye, Sachai pe Bharosa Kijiye."*
- **Speaker**: *"Millions of first-time retail investors, elderly citizens, and regional language speakers in India receive viral WhatsApp voice notes and Telegram tips promising 300% guaranteed returns or fake IPO allotments. Without accessible fact-checking tools, billions of rupees are lost to cyber fraudsters every year. Introducing SACH AI — the Smart Assistant for Checking Honesty in Financial Information, built for SANGYAN Hackathon Track E."*

### Scene 2: Multilingual & Accessibility Demonstration (0:45 – 1:15)
- **Visual**: Toggle language between English, हिन्दी (Hindi), and বাংলা (Bengali). Toggle **Large Text Mode (A+)** and **High-Contrast Mode**.
- **Speaker**: *"SACH AI is designed for complete digital inclusivity. With one click, users can switch between English, Hindi, and Bengali, or activate high-contrast and enlarged typography for elderly and low-vision users."*

### Scene 3: Voice Ingestion & Audio Transcription (1:15 – 2:10)
- **Visual**: Click Microphone in Verification Workspace. Speak: *"VIP Telegram group guarantee 300% profit in 5 days, buy XYZ Power now."* Click Stop. Show real-time transcription and editable transcript box.
- **Speaker**: *"Our voice-first pipeline captures microphone speech or uploaded voice notes, transcribes the speech in real-time, and lets the user review and edit the transcript before analysis, with zero persistent storage of sensitive personal audio."*

### Scene 4: AI & Rule-Based Scam Analysis with Spoken Explanation (2:10 – 3:30)
- **Visual**: Click 'Analyze for Misinformation'. Dashboard appears with **Potential Red Flags Detected (Risk Score: 65/100)**, Claim-by-Claim Breakdown highlighting trigger words, Red Flags Cards, and Official SEBI/NSDL/Cybercrime Evidence links. Click **"Listen to Explanation"** and play audio readout.
- **Speaker**: *"Our hybrid AI engine segments each claim, scans for regulatory violation patterns like prohibited assured returns and operator pumps, and synthesizes a simple plain-language explanation with an audio readout. Notice how SACH AI automatically links directly to SEBI SCORES, NSDL depository verification, and the 1930 Cybercrime helpline."*

### Scene 5: Investor Education & 1930 Emergency Reporting (3:30 – 4:30)
- **Visual**: Navigate to Investor Safety section. Show Common Scam Patterns, interactive awareness quiz, and the 4-step emergency incident reporting workflow.
- **Speaker**: *"Beyond detection, SACH AI empowers investors through interactive quizzes and actionable guides on utilizing SEBI SCORES and calling the National Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 within the golden hour to freeze fraudulent transactions."*

### Scene 6: Conclusion & Impact (4:30 – 5:00)
- **Visual**: Return to Hero Section with SEBI & NSDL alignment and summary metrics.
- **Speaker**: *"SACH AI transforms investor protection from passive warnings into an active, voice-first shield for India's next 100 million investors. Suniye, Samajhiye, Sachai pe Bharosa Kijiye. Thank you."*
